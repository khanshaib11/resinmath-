How to Price Resin Work Without Losing Money

Most makers undercharge because they only add up resin and wood, completely forgetting the cost of consumables (sandpaper discs, mixing pails, polishing compound, PPE) and their own skilled shop hours.

Resin Rate = Kit Price ÷ Kit Volume
Resin Used Cost = Required Volume × Resin Rate
Total Materials = Resin Used + Wood + Consumables
Production Cost = Materials + (Labor Hours × Hourly Wage)
Retail Price = Production Cost × (1 + Markup %)

Frequently asked questions

How much does epoxy resin cost per gallon?

Quality deep-pour epoxy typically costs between $90 and $150 per gallon when purchased in 1.5 to 3 gallon kits. Premium tabletop coating epoxies range from $70 to $120 per gallon.

How should I price handmade resin tables and crafts?

Standard maker pricing is: (Total Materials + Consumables + [Hours × Hourly Wage]) × 1.3 to 1.5 for Wholesale, and × 2.0 to 2.5 for Direct Retail. Never price based on resin cost alone.

How do I calculate kit purchase requirement?

Always divide your required pour volume by the kit size and round UP to the next integer. You cannot buy 0.4 of an epoxy kit.
