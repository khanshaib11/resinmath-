How much epoxy resin do I need?

Resin is sold by volume, so every project comes down to one question: how many cubic inches (or cm³) am I filling? Multiply length × width × depth to get the volume, then convert it: 1 US fl oz is 1.805 in³, 1 US gallon is 231 in³, and 1 ml is exactly 1 cm³. Most makers add 10–15% extra for what stays behind in cups and what drips off the edges.
