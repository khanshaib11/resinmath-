Mold volume formulas

ShapeFormulaCommon use
Cylinderπ × r² × hCoasters, trinket dishes
BoxL × W × HTrays, blocks, bookends
Sphere4⁄3 × π × r³Orbs, paperweights
Dome2⁄3 × π × r³Domes, keepsakes
Cone1⁄3 × π × r² × hPyramids*, cone decor

r = diameter ÷ 2. *A square pyramid is L × W × H ÷ 3, which is about 27% more than a cone of the same width.

The water method for odd shapes

  1. Fill the mold to the brim with water.
  2. Pour the water into a measuring cup and read the volume.
  3. Enter that number above, then dry the mold completely. Even a drop of water clouds epoxy.

Frequently asked questions

How do I measure the volume of an irregular silicone mold?

Use the water method: fill the mold with water, pour it into a measuring cup, and note the volume. Dry the mold completely before pouring resin, because water ruins epoxy.

How much resin do I need for a 4 inch coaster?

A 4 inch round coaster that is ¼ inch thick holds about 3.1 cubic inches, which is roughly 1.7 fl oz or 52 ml. Add about 10% for mixing loss.

How much resin for a sphere mold?

Volume equals 4⁄3 × π × radius³. A 2 inch (5 cm) sphere holds about 4.2 cubic inches, or around 2.3 fl oz (69 ml).
